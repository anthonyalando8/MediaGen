──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STRUCTURE — MANDATORY
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Beat 1 opens by naming the question or idea the video answers — curiosity,
   not shock. No manufactured outrage; this is an explainer, not a reel.
2. The middle beats develop the idea in ORDER: cause → mechanism → implication,
   or problem → why it happens → what to do about it. Each beat should teach
   ONE new piece of the picture — don't restate the previous beat.
3. The final beat is a clear takeaway or a practical next step — not a
   "follow for more" CTA.
4. Beat count: 8-16, matched to how many distinct steps the topic actually
   has. Don't pad to hit a number.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WRITING RULES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Every beat text is 12-30 words. Total across all beats: under 170 words.
- Explain plainly. Use "you" where natural, but prioritize CLARITY over hype.
- BANNED PHRASES: "here's the thing" / "the truth is" / "most people" /
  "follow for more" / "let's dive in".
- Optional: wrap ONE word per sentence in *asterisks* to mark the landing word.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CINEMATIC FIELDS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Give the video at least 3 different `camera` values and at least 2 `pace`
  values across the whole script. Don't run the same camera 4+ beats in a row.
- camera: hook=push_in; explanatory beats=static; payoff/cta=pull_out.
- transition: cut as the neutral default between explanatory beats,
  dip_black/fade into the payoff.
- intensity: hook ~0.7-0.8, explanatory beats ~0.45-0.65 (this is a calmer
  format than a TikTok reel — don't push everything to 0.9), payoff ~0.7.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VISUAL QUERY — REQUIRED, must return a CONTEXTUAL image
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Image search is LITERAL — it ignores emotion and metaphor. Every visual_query:
1. LEADS WITH the global `subject` (or a concrete same-domain noun) — the
   literal thing on screen, present in EVERY beat.
2. Adds at most 1-2 VISUAL modifiers (lighting, angle, setting).
3. NEVER uses feeling/abstraction words ("struggle", "journey", "freedom") —
   they return generic nature/abstract stock instead of your topic.
4. 2-4 words. Concrete + visual. Template: [subject] + [setting or lighting].
