──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STRUCTURE — MANDATORY
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Beat 1 is the HOOK: name the promise and the count ("Five mistakes killing
   your focus — number three is you"). Create curiosity or mild stakes.
2. Each following beat is ONE list item, in order. Its keyword LEADS WITH THE
   NUMBER ("#1 …", "#2 …"). Body delivers the item in one or two tight sentences.
3. The FINAL beat is a payoff or challenge — not a "follow for more" CTA.
4. Item count: 3-8 items (so 4-10 beats total). Match what the topic needs.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WRITING RULES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Every beat text is 10-28 words. Total across all beats: under 240 words.
- Speak as a creator with an opinion. Use "you". Be concrete and specific.
- Keep items PARALLEL in shape (all "do X" or all "avoid Y"), so the rundown reads clean.
- BANNED PHRASES: "here's the thing" / "the truth is" / "most people" / "follow for more".
- Optional: wrap ONE word per sentence in *asterisks* to mark the landing word.
  (Not required for this format, but it improves the on-screen emphasis animation.)

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CINEMATIC FIELDS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Give the video at least 2 different `camera` values and at least 2 `layout`
  values across the whole script. Item beats MAY share a look, but don't run the
  same camera for more than 3 beats in a row.
- camera: hook=push_in or snap_zoom; items=static or push_in; payoff=pull_out.
- transition: slam_cut into the hook, cut/whip_pan between items, fade/dip_black on the payoff.
- intensity: hook ~0.9, items ~0.6-0.75, payoff ~0.8.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VISUAL QUERY — REQUIRED, must return a CONTEXTUAL image
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Image search is LITERAL — it ignores emotion and metaphor. Every visual_query:
1. LEADS WITH the global `subject` (or a concrete same-domain noun) — the literal
   thing on screen, present in EVERY beat.
2. Adds at most 1-2 VISUAL modifiers (lighting, angle, setting): "night", "close up",
   "macro", "overhead", "office", "screen glow".
3. NEVER uses feeling/abstraction words ("struggle", "journey", "freedom", "mind") —
   they return generic nature/abstract stock instead of your topic.
4. 2-4 words. Concrete + visual. Template: [subject] + [setting or lighting].

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FINAL CHECKLIST (rewrite if any fail):
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Beat 1 = hook naming the count; last beat = payoff/challenge (not a follow CTA).
2. Item beats numbered in their keyword ("#1", "#2", …), in order.
3. Each beat 10-28 words; total under 240.
4. ≥2 cameras and ≥2 layouts across the script; no camera repeated 4+ beats in a row.
5. Every beat has a numeric intensity in [0.0, 1.0].
6. Every visual_query is subject-first with no mood/abstraction words.
