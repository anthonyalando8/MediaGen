──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION STRUCTURE — MANDATORY
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The TOP LEVEL has "sections", NOT a flat "beats" array. Each section is:
  {{ "id": "<slug>", "pacing_arc": "build|steady|rise_fall|wind_down", "beats": [ ...same beat shape as short-form... ] }}

1. "intro" section — `pacing_arc: "build"`. 3-5 beats. Names the question this
   video answers and why it matters. Ends with a beat that previews the
   sections to come (a light "here's what we'll cover", stated plainly, not
   as a banned hype phrase).
2. 2-3 BODY sections, each `pacing_arc: "steady"`, each covering ONE distinct
   sub-point of the topic, 4-8 beats each. Give each a descriptive `id`
   (e.g. "why-it-happens", "the-mechanism", "what-to-do"). Sections build on
   each other in logical order — don't restate an earlier section.
3. "conclusion" section — `pacing_arc: "wind_down"`. 3-5 beats. Synthesizes
   the body sections into a clear takeaway or practical next step. NOT a
   "follow for more" CTA.
4. Total beats across ALL sections: 18-36. Total words: 480-750. Match the
   count to how many genuine sub-points the topic has — don't pad a section
   to hit a number, and don't cram unrelated sub-points into one section.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BEAT `type` — REQUIRED ON EVERY BEAT, AND VARIED
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
`type` is not decoration: it drives camera/pace/emotion defaults AND how the
beat's text is laid out on screen. A script where every body beat is "insight"
renders as the same card for five minutes. Rules:

- EVERY beat carries an explicit `type`. Never omit it.
- Across the whole script use at least FIVE distinct types.
- No more than THREE "insight" beats in a row. Break up long explanatory runs
  with the type that matches what the beat is actually doing:
    insight  — explains a mechanism or fact (the default body beat)
    tension  — names a problem, risk, or cost
    truth    — states a principle or a quotable claim
    flip     — corrects a misconception, or pivots "but actually…"
    climax   — the single most consequential beat of a body section
    payoff   — resolves a section into its practical consequence
- "hook" is the FIRST beat only. "cta" is the LAST beat only.
- Each body section should contain at least two different types.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STRUCTURED BEATS — `items` (use on 3-6 beats per script, no more)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Some beats read far better as structure than as a sentence. When a beat is
GENUINELY one of the shapes below, add an `items` array alongside its `text`.
The `text` stays the spoken narration; `items` is what appears on screen.

- An ENUMERATION (2-5 parallel things) — `type: "insight"` or `"payoff"`:
    "items": [{{"text": "Clocks drift"}}, {{"text": "Packets vanish"}}, {{"text": "Retries duplicate"}}]
  Each item ≤ 6 words. Only when the items are truly parallel — not a sentence
  chopped at its commas.

- FIGURES worth showing as numbers — `type: "insight"` or `"truth"`:
    "items": [{{"label": "40%", "text": "of outages start here"}}, {{"label": "3x", "text": "longer to detect"}}]
  2-4 items, each `label` a short figure. Use ONLY with real numbers from the
  topic — never invent statistics.

Also, where it's natural (do NOT force these):
- Put a short verbatim claim in "double quotes" inside `text` on one or two
  `truth` beats — those render as a pull quote.
- On `insight` beats that introduce a term, make `keyword` THE TERM itself
  (2-3 words, no verb) and let `text` define it — those render as a
  definition card.

Omit `items` on every beat that isn't one of these shapes. A forced list is
worse than a clean sentence.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
WRITING RULES (same as every format)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Every beat text is 12-30 words.
- Explain plainly. Prioritize clarity over hype — this is long-form, the
  viewer already chose to watch 5 minutes; don't manufacture urgency.
- BANNED PHRASES: "here's the thing" / "the truth is" / "most people" /
  "follow for more" / "let's dive in".

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CINEMATIC FIELDS & VISUAL QUERY
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Same rules as the 1-minute explainer format: ≥3 cameras and ≥2 paces across
the WHOLE script (all sections combined), no camera repeated 4+ beats running.
`intensity` should roughly track each section's `pacing_arc`: "build" sections
climb from ~0.5 to ~0.75, "steady" sections hold ~0.5-0.65, "wind_down" eases
from ~0.6 down to ~0.45 by the final beat. Every `visual_query` is a concrete
same-domain noun, 2-4 words, no mood words — prefer the noun closest to THIS
beat's specific content over reusing the global `subject` string on every beat
(see the worked example below: none of its queries repeat "server room"
verbatim, they each pick the concrete noun that matches THAT beat).
