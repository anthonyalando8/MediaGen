Write a HOW-TO / TUTORIAL video script — a procedure a viewer can actually follow.

TOPIC: "{topic}"

Return ONLY valid JSON. No text before or after. No markdown. No code fences.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STRUCTURE — the spine, in this order
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1  beat_01        type "hook"     — the OUTCOME and why the usual way fails.
                                       Do not list the steps here.
  2  beat_02        type "insight"  — WHAT YOU NEED. Carries an `items` array.
                                       Omit this beat entirely if the topic
                                       needs nothing but attention.
  3  N step beats   type "insight"  — ONE action per beat, counting UP (1 → N)
  4  one beat       type "tension"  — THE COMMON MISTAKE. What people get wrong
                                       and what it costs. Placed after the steps,
                                       not among them.
  5  one beat       type "payoff"   — how you know it worked. The observable result.
  6  final beat     type "cta"      — what to try next, or a question. Not "subscribe".

N is 4-8. Use the number of steps the procedure genuinely has.

⚠ Count UP. Step 1 is first. (This is the opposite of a countdown — do not rank
  by importance, order by SEQUENCE.)
⚠ ONE action per step beat. If a step contains "and then", it is two steps.
⚠ Steps must be in the order a person actually does them. A step that depends on
  a later step is the worst failure this format has.
⚠ Every step beat is typed "insight". That is correct and intentional — do not
  vary the type for the sake of variety. Visual variety comes from the per-beat
  camera/layout/background fields below.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP KEYWORDS — REQUIRED on every step beat
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Step beats' `keyword` must begin with the step number, then the action in 2-3
words. Lead with a VERB.

  "STEP 1 FIND THE BEVEL" / "STEP 2 WET THE STONE" / "STEP 5 STROP THE EDGE"

Uppercase. No trailing punctuation. The number prefix is not decoration — the
renderer treats a number-led keyword as the frame's hero text and will never
give that beat a textless composition, so a step without it can lose its label
entirely.

hook / needs / mistake / payoff / cta keywords carry NO number:
  "SHARP IN FIVE MINUTES" / "WHAT YOU NEED" / "EVERYONE SKIPS THIS"
  / "THE PAPER TEST" / "TRY IT ON ONE KNIFE"

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STRUCTURED BEATS — `items` on the WHAT YOU NEED beat
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The materials list is a genuine enumeration and reads far better as a list than
as a sentence:

    "items": [{{"text": "Whetstone, 1000 grit"}}, {{"text": "A damp towel"}},
              {{"text": "Ten flat minutes"}}]

2-4 entries, each ≤ 5 words, each a real thing. The `text` field stays the spoken
narration ("You need three things, and one of them is patience.").

Do NOT put `items` on a step beat — a step is one action, and splitting it into
bullets makes it look like several. Do not put `items` anywhere else.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STRICT RULES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TEXT: every beat 14-28 words. Total across all beats: 160-380.

TEXT ON A STEP BEAT: say what to DO, then the tell that it's working. Imperative
mood. Do not restate the keyword — it is already on screen.
  Good, keyword "STEP 2 WET THE STONE":
    "Soak it until the bubbles stop rising. A dry stone *glazes* and stops cutting
     within a minute."
  Bad (restates the keyword, adds nothing):
    "Step two is to wet the *stone* before you begin."

BE SPECIFIC. Numbers, angles, times, temperatures, counts — whatever the topic
really has. "A few minutes" is a failure; "about ten minutes" is a step.
Never invent a figure you aren't sure of; say the observable tell instead.

TONE: someone who has done this many times showing you, not a manual reading
itself aloud. Use "you". No hedging, no encouragement padding.

BANNED PHRASES: "here's the thing" / "the truth is" / "in today's world"
                "let's dive in" / "without further ado" / "it's that simple"
                "and that's all there is to it" / "make sure to" / "don't forget to"
                "follow for more" / "in this video"

⚠ Never narrate the video's own structure. No "first I'll show you", no
  "now that we've covered", no "in the next step". The step number on screen
  already carries the sequence.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PER-BEAT VARIETY — MANDATORY
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Every step beat shares a type and a purpose, so if the visual fields don't move
the whole middle of the video is one frame with changing words. Across the script:
   1. At least 3 different `camera` values
   2. At least 2 different `pace` values
   3. At least 2 different `layout` values
   4. At least 2 different `background` values
   5. Never the same `camera` on 3 consecutive beats
   6. Consecutive STEP beats must differ in at least TWO of
      camera / layout / background
Rewrite before outputting if any fail.

Keep the motion SMALL. This format allows push_in, pull_out, tilt_up, handheld,
micro_shake and static — it does not allow snap_zoom, and transitions are limited
to cut / blur_wipe / fade / dip_black. A viewer copying what they see needs a
stable frame; showiness here reads as amateur, not energetic.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VISUAL QUERY — REQUIRED, must return a CONTEXTUAL image
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Image search is LITERAL. Mood words ("precision mastery focus") return generic
stock — nature and abstract dominate the pool — so a knife-sharpening video ends
up showing a mountain. Over-niche queries return nothing and fall back to a
random photo.

This format has the easiest job of any: each step ACTS ON A PHYSICAL THING. Name it.

RULES (all mandatory):
1. THE OBJECT OF THIS STEP, concretely. Step 2 wets a stone → "whetstone water
   soaking". Step 5 tests the edge → "knife blade paper cut". Only fall back to
   the global `subject` when a step has no object of its own.
2. Then AT MOST 1-2 visual modifiers: "close up", "macro", "overhead", "hands",
   "kitchen", "workbench", "daylight".
3. "hands" is unusually useful here and mostly absent from other formats — a
   pair of hands doing the thing is the single most on-topic tutorial image
   available. Use it on 2-3 step beats.
4. BANNED: every feeling and abstraction — "precision", "mastery", "focus",
   "patience", "journey", "craft", "perfection", "satisfying", "zen".
5. 2-4 words. Concrete + visual.

⚠ Every step beat needs a DIFFERENT query. Repeating "chef knife close up" with
  a new adjective five times gives one photo for the whole procedure.

For a KNIFE-SHARPENING topic (subject "chef knife"):
  Good: "whetstone water soaking" / "hands sharpening knife angle" / "knife edge
        macro closeup" / "blade paper slicing test" / "leather strop workbench"
  Bad:  "chef knife" ×5 / "precision craft" / "satisfying sharpness" / "mastery"

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
INTENSITY — 0.0 to 1.0, REQUIRED on every beat
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A procedure is EVEN. It does not build to a peak — it arrives at a result. Keep
the whole middle in a narrow band; the only lift is the mistake beat.

  hook:            0.72 - 0.80
  what you need:   0.40 - 0.50
  step beats:      0.48 - 0.62      flat across all of them, ±0.06 at most
  the mistake:     0.72 - 0.82      the one moment with real pressure
  payoff:          0.58 - 0.68
  cta:             0.55 - 0.65

⚠ No beat above 0.85. There is no climax in a tutorial; a frame that slams while
  someone is holding a knife is a bad video.
⚠ Step intensities must stay within 0.14 of each other. A rising staircase makes
  step 6 feel more important than step 2, which is false — every step is required.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PATTERN INTERRUPT — leave "" on essentially every beat
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Someone following along has their hands busy and their eyes on a detail. A
frame-level disruption makes them lose their place. At most ONE in the whole
script, on the mistake beat, and only if that beat is ≥ 0.80:

  ""       → none. This is the correct value for every beat but possibly one.
  "iris"   → vignette tightens. The only tasteful option here (the mistake beat).
  "freeze" → motion pauses. Acceptable on the mistake beat as an alternative.

Never "slam", "chroma", "invert", "tilt" or "flash" in this format.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
COMPOSITION — optional, "" usually. At most ONCE per script.
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ""         → standard (use on every beat unless calling)
  "crop-low" → text sits at bottom 30% — good when the object needs the frame
  "sparse"   → keyword takes 35% width, rest empty
  "corner"   → text bottom-right, max negative space

Use on a step beat whose image deserves room. Never "tilt" — a dutch angle on an
instructional frame is disorienting.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
EMPHASIS WORDS — REQUIRED, exactly one per sentence
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Wrap ONE word or short phrase per sentence in *asterisks* — the word the
listener must land on. In this format it should usually be the TELL or the
CONSEQUENCE, not the action.

  "Soak it until the bubbles stop. A dry stone *glazes* and stops cutting."
  "Hold the spine two coins off the surface — about *fifteen degrees*."
  "If it tears the paper instead of slicing, the burr is still *attached*."

FINAL CHECKLIST before outputting (rewrite if any fail):
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1.  Spine correct: hook → (needs) → steps ASCENDING → mistake → (payoff) → cta.
2.  Every step beat's keyword starts with "STEP <n>" and leads with a verb.
3.  No non-step keyword carries a number prefix.
4.  Steps are in real execution order; no step depends on a later one.
5.  Exactly one action per step beat — no "and then".
6.  Every step beat is typed "insight".
7.  Word count: each beat 14-28, total 160-380.
8.  Every text field has EXACTLY one *emphasis word* per sentence.
9.  No step text restates its own keyword.
10. All step intensities within 0.14 of each other; no beat above 0.85.
11. ≥3 cameras, ≥2 paces, ≥2 layouts, ≥2 backgrounds; no camera 3-in-a-row.
12. Consecutive step beats differ in ≥2 of camera / layout / background.
13. No snap_zoom; no slam/chroma/invert/tilt/flash interrupts.
14. Every visual_query is a concrete object, ≤4 words, no mood words, and all
    step queries are DIFFERENT objects. 2-3 of them use "hands".
15. `items` appears on the WHAT YOU NEED beat only, if at all.
16. No banned phrase, and no narration of the video's own structure.

Output the JSON only.
